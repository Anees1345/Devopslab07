Research Limitation Notice: The model was trained on 35 verified records. Evaluation results are preliminary and may vary substantially with additional data. They do not guarantee real-world prediction accuracy across newly launched market vehicles.
Metric 1

Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

Measures the average magnitude of prediction errors in the original currency units (PKR). All individual differences are weighted equally. Lower MAE is better.

Metric 2

Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

Square root of the average squared errors. Because errors are squared before averaging, RMSE heavily penalizes large outlier mistakes. Lower RMSE is better.

Metric 3

Coefficient of Determination (R²)

Proportion of variance in EV price explained by the features relative to a naive mean baseline. A score of 1.0 is perfect; negative scores indicate worse performance than a simple average. Higher R² is better.

Why Random Forest Was Selected Over Linear Regression

The dataset features non-linear interactions: battery chemistry (Lithium-Ion vs. Lead Acid) combined with motor power (Watts) creates steep non-linear pricing step functions in Pakistan. Linear Regression suffered severely from multi-collinearity after One-Hot Encoding categorical features on a small dataset (resulting in a negative R² of -4.651).

In contrast, the ensemble tree structure of Random Forest Regressor (300 estimators, min_samples_leaf=2) prevented extreme variance, captured categorical splits organically, and achieved the lowest pooled out-of-fold MAE (PKR 64,112).
